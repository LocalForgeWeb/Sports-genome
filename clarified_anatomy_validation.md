# Clarified Anatomy Layout Validation

The live Body Lab now displays front and back anatomy side by side on the desktop analysis board. Each side uses a centered vector figure, a separate header and reset control, six left-perimeter and six right-perimeter callouts, and visible inward connector cues. Both the figure regions and the callout cards remain interactive.

The front side shows callouts from clavicular pectoralis major through hip adductors; the posterior side covers upper and middle trapezius, posterior deltoid, infraspinatus, latissimus dorsi, triceps heads, forearm extensors, erector spinae, gluteus maximus, hamstrings, and gastrocnemius. The layout is original while following the user-requested centered-figure and surrounding-callout information hierarchy.

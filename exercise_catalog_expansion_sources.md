# Cable-Focused Sport Exercise Expansion

The catalog now contains a 100-record expansion: **80 cable exercises** and **20 familiar athletic, landmine, kettlebell, plyometric, sled, and battle-rope options**. Every record is normalized into the app’s existing muscle, movement, equipment, quality, and sport-fit structure. Sport-fit labels express movement-based training rationale, rather than a guarantee of performance or injury outcomes.

## Research basis

| Focus area | Sources used | Catalog application |
|---|---|---|

| Power and unilateral lower-body training | [Moran et al. bilateral/unilateral systematic review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-020-01367-9); [upper-body plyometric meta-analysis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40798-023-00631-2) | Plyometric, unilateral, landmine, medicine-ball, and power-quality labels. |

## Data integrity checks

The expansion test verifies exactly 100 added records, 80 cable options, 400 total catalog IDs, unique expansion names, no expansion-name overlap with the base catalog, and complete sport-fit objects. This preserves integration with existing catalog search, recommendation scoring, movement coverage, warm-up inference, and training-day planning.

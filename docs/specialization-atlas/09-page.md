# Specialization atlas: the page (16 Oct 2026; review fixes, follow-ups and the final review's phone fixes 17 Oct)

Body Lab → **Specialize** is the forearm page the brief asks for (§6). This note records what was built, where, and the choices made inside the design (`02-design.md` §7–§8, §12).

## Ways in, and Back

- The Body Lab tab row: Movements · Muscles · Exercises · **Specialize**. The bottom dock stays at four.
- Muscles (the body map): selecting the forearm (`forearms` or `brachioradialis` key) adds **Explore forearm coverage** under the map's next steps. A key that names exactly one muscle (`brachioradialis`) opens the page with that muscle selected (`nodeForBodyMapKey` on the region).
- Review → Week → a forearm muscle's detail: **Explore forearm coverage**, which opens the page in **Planned** mode on the week Review is reading (`mode=planned&pweek=N`).
- Search: **Forearm specialization** (terms include forearm, grip, wrist, brachioradialis, specialize).

An entry point that names where it was pressed gets **Back to <that page>** at the top of the page. The origin rides in `history.state`, so it survives a reload or iOS restoring the app, and tapping the active tab again keeps it. The tab, the dock and search are not "opened from" anywhere and get no Back.

## Address

`?workspace=specialization&region=forearm` plus, only when they differ from the defaults: `node`, `limb` (left), `window` (`7d`, `custom` with `from`/`to`), `mode` (planned), `view`, `layer`, `map` (target), `pweek`. Entering the page pushes a history entry; changes on the page replace it. Every other page strips these parameters, and a hand-edited value that does not validate is dropped from the address on load; the page also drops, in one change, a muscle the region does not have, a plan week that is not built, and a view the drawing does not have (or its first view, which the address leaves out) (tested in `Home.specialization.test.ts` and `SpecializationPanel.render.test.ts`).

## Choices made inside the design

- **What the map paints.** Volume mode paints *direct work bouts*: bouts in which the muscle is the target, a direct dynamic set or a deliberate hold, each counted once (a mixed bout counts when one of its parts is direct). Holds are bouts here, never seconds turned into sets. Contributing and incidental work stays off the map and on the list and ledger, so grip during rows does not light up the finger flexors as if trained. The key says this in words.
- **Bins.** Fixed: 0 · 1–2 · 3–5 · 6–9 · 10–14 · 15+, on `--sg-exposure-1..5`, never rescaled to the busiest muscle. Zero is a dashed resting fill ("Mapped, no direct work"); no reviewed mapping is hatched ("Mapping not reviewed"). Target mode: under 25 % · 25–49 % · 50–74 % · 75–99 % · 100 % and over, from the athlete's own targets; a muscle without one is unpainted ("Not targeted"), and with several targets the furthest behind decides the step.
- **Findings, in order:** a target the athlete set and is behind on; sets with no arm; work tagged with the region but not mapped to a muscle; a mapping under review; muscles with no reviewed mapping ("missing mapping, not missing training"); a difference of two or more direct bouts between the arms, stated in numbers ("Two-handed sets count for both arms; only one-arm sets make the arms differ"); strapped sets that earned no grip credit. Nothing is scored, and nothing suggests work only to fill a muscle. A muscle target opens the muscle's exercises, a movement target opens the movement ("See its sets and exercises"), and the no-arm and region-only findings open their sets ("See these sets").
- **Every total opens its sets** (brief §1). A muscle's numbers open its ledger (exercise → bout → segment) in the muscle sheet. The region's totals open a sheet of their own (`BoutListSheet`): the distinct bouts behind the strip's count (with the no-arm bouts listed apart), the sets with no arm recorded, the work tagged with the region and mapped to no muscle, one exercise's bouts from Exercises in this window, one day's bouts from its bar there (the same distinct bouts on the arm the bar counts, titled with the day; `regionBoutRowsOnDay`), and the window line's pair (this week's logged bouts beside the plan week's, each list headed with the count the line gave), each logged bout with **Open this workout**. A day with no work is not a button: it has nothing to open. The calculator keeps those rows (`regionBoutRows`, `regionUnknownSideRows`, `regionUnclassifiedRows` on the summary; `06-calculation.md` §2).
  - **Progress's weekly bars.** Under the "Distinct bouts per week" chart, each week is a button with its Monday and its count, two rows of four, oldest first (eight 44 px buttons do not fit one row on a phone; at 320 each is 58.7 × 47.8 px). A week with work opens that week's region bouts on the arm in the same sheet ("Week of 12 Oct", "12–18 Oct: 14 distinct bouts, every set that loaded a muscle of the forearms on the right arm …"), exercise → bout → segment with **Open this workout**; the count on the button and the rows are the same distinct bouts (`regionBoutRows` of that week's summary). A week with none is not a button.
  - **"Not counted" in the muscle sheet.** Each bout a mapping matched that earned the muscle nothing (a strapped set's grip, a mapping under review) is drawn as the ledger draws a counted one: the exercise, the workout, the day and the arm (and "matched by name" when it was), its segments as logged, why it was not counted, and **Open this workout**.
  - **The completeness counts** in How this is counted. The ones that have their own list open it: "See the 3 sets for the forearms only" and "See the 1 set with no arm recorded", the same sheets the findings open. The rest say plainly where they are or that they cannot be opened: sets under review are listed under Not counted in each muscle's sheet; sets matched by name are among the others, marked "matched by name"; sets with no arm recorded fall in three places, and the line says which: those that loaded a muscle of the region are the no-arm list; those tagged with the region and mapped to no muscle are in the region-only list ("3 sets with no arm recorded are among the sets for the forearms only, and open there."; such a set has an arm only when one was recorded, since no variant says the exercise is two-handed); the rest count for no muscle of the region, and the line says how many and that no list of them opens there; sets not completed and warm-ups are left out of every total and not kept as a list, so they cannot be opened.
- **Movements** (brief §7, "Targets attach to muscle or action"). A movement target's group in Targets has **Open**, and its finding opens the same sheet (`MovementSheet`): what the movement is and which of the region's muscles do it (each with Open); its numbers for the window and arm, counted as a movement target counts them (distinct bouts in which a credited mapping for the movement on a region muscle is that kind of work, hold seconds once per bout, no-arm bouts apart); its targets with their progress; the bouts behind the numbers, one row per bout however many muscles moved it, with the kinds of work and the muscles, **Open this workout**, and Why this counts for each exercise; and exercises for the movement (`recommendFor({ target: { actionId } })`) with Add or swap, About it and Not for me. Add or swap opens the add sheet; its preview leads with the region muscle the chosen exercise is credited with for that movement.
- **Why this counts** (brief §2–§4). Under each exercise in a ledger and each suggestion, closed until opened: for each reviewed mapping behind it, the muscle and movement, the relationship and mode ("Direct target, moves the joint"), what the counting rule makes of it ("Counted as direct dynamic"), what it rests on (Inferred from anatomy, Anatomy reference, Biomechanics, Measured by EMG, Measured in training studies), the written rationale, and each source with where in it and its limit. A closing line says the kind of work is a Sports Genome counting choice and that the sources speak to what a muscle does, not to how much it grows.
- **Planned mode while the plan loads.** No summary is shown until the plan has loaded: no zeros, no hatching, no target progress and no findings. The drawing is plain, the rows and the strip say "Loading your plan…", and so do the findings, the window's exercises and the sheets; the muscle sheet's target section waits too, rather than show each target at 0.
- **The clock.** The page's `now` is refreshed when the page is looked at again (`visibilitychange`) and passed to every summary, so each target's own period (a rolling 7 days, a calendar week) and whether a target is active follow the clock, not the moment the page was opened.
- **What is suggested.** Each suggestion has **Not for me**, which leaves that exercise out at once, and the sheet's "What is suggested" lists the equipment among the suggestions as toggles and the exercises left out, each with "Suggest again". Stored on the device under the account's key (`lib/specialization/exclusions.ts`) and passed to the recommender. The message and its Undo ("Lever Ulnar Deviation will not be suggested. Undo") take the removed row's place in the list, so they are where the athlete's thumb was, not after every remaining suggestion; focus moves to Undo (the button that had it is gone), which is read with the message, and after Undo to the restored exercise's Not for me (or, when it is no longer among the five shown, to a line saying it is suggested again). An exercise brought back by "Suggest again" drops its Undo. "Suggest again" takes its own row away, so focus moves to the next exercise left out, or to the disclosure's summary when none is left, never the page body; a refused write is said, as it is for Not for me. Changing the equipment clears the "suggested again" line, so it never appears later about an earlier change. Measured with FCU's first suggestion tapped at the bottom of the visible sheet: the Undo lands at 738–782 in a sheet body visible from 204 to 844 at 390 × 844, at 542–586 (193–667) at 375 × 667 and at 444–488 (208–568) at 320 × 568, 51.8 × 44 px, with focus on it (`not-for-me-undo-*.png`). The final review had measured it more than 1,000 px below the visible sheet, with focus on the page body.
- **Said once above the exercises** (`Suggestions.tsx`, design §8). A target of the athlete's that no reviewed exercise can count toward, the recommender's `targetNote` ("No reviewed exercise for flexor carpi radialis counts toward your target of 3 deliberate hold bouts this week."); when there is no candidate at all, the empty reason already leads with it, so it is not said twice. Then, in the muscle sheet, a main action of the muscle with no direct work that no candidate covers, in one sentence with why (`missingActionNote` in `model.ts`, worked out as `recommendFor` decides): no reviewed exercise trains it directly yet ("Supination, a main action of this muscle, has no direct work in this window, and no reviewed exercise trains it directly yet.", the biceps after palms-up curls); the athlete left those exercises out, by exercise or by equipment (the page hands the sheet the catalog's equipment, so it can tell); or they answer a target already met ("… answer a target you have already met, so none is suggested."). Each is a fact about the records and the mapping, with no shortfall in it. A candidate that covers the missing action leads its own "why" with it instead, and then nothing is said above.
- **Already covered by**, as a list in words: each exercise with the plan days it is on, then the sets logged in this window ("Hammer Curl: planned on Day 02 · Pull; 3 sets logged in this window"). The recommender carries logged work as an extra entry in `AlreadyCovered.days` (key `logged`); `coveredWords` says it as the phrase it is, never as a day.
- **Hold time not recorded.** A hold logged before the logger kept seconds reads "time not recorded", not "0 s".
- **Targets.** "Both arms" saves two targets, one per arm. Each target can be changed (its number) or ended (it stops counting from today; one that started today is removed), and an ended target moves to "Ended targets" with its dates. Removing or ending offers one Undo for everything removed since the last other action; focus moves to it, and it is brought into view clear of the bottom dock and the sticky tab row by measuring them (`reveal.ts`), since a `scroll-margin` alone is ignored for an Undo already on screen under the dock. Measured in the Targets disclosure with Remove tapped at y 440 and 480 at 390 × 844, 520 at 375 × 667 and 430 at 320 × 568: the Undo's bottom sits 11.8–12.5 px above the dock's top every time, and the element at its centre is the Undo itself (`targets-undo-*.png`). The page's Targets disclosure (`TargetsOverview.tsx`) lists every target of the region by muscle or movement with this arm's progress, and adds targets for a muscle or for a movement (brief §7: "Targets attach to muscle or action"); the muscle sheet's editor (`TargetsEditor.tsx`) shares the same form with its muscle fixed. The editor says a target is intent, not a proven threshold, and that numbers above 15 are fine.
- **Add or swap.** The exercise (the muscle's candidates, with equipment and whether it is already in the week chosen in the sheet), the week, the day (never preselected: Save waits for it), Add with where it goes (First, After an exercise, or At the end) or Swap with one already there (it keeps that exercise's place, prescription and settings), then planned totals before → after for every muscle that changes, the selected one first, plus the region's distinct bouts. Nothing pins a default prescription: an entry without its own reads as the goal's default for its place in the day, as every plan reader does, and the sheet says when an insert moves an unprescribed exercise to a place with a different default. Save hands Home `{ week, dayKey, slotIndex, catalogExerciseId, replaceEntryId?, insertAt? }`; Home applies the same pure edit (`applyPlanChangeToRecord`), refuses with a reason (plan loading, week not built, already in that day, the swap target gone), and shows a toast naming every option, with Undo that takes back that one change wherever the day now lives (never adding an exercise a second time). The page stays where it was.
- **Progress.** Circumference and dynamometer grip by arm, with landmark, distance, posture, pumped or not, device, and the date and time of the reading (a time is required for a reading on an earlier day). Units follow the profile: inches and lb for an lb profile, cm and kg otherwise; storage stays in cm and newtons. Grip has its own chart and never shares a scale with exercise load. Weekly region bouts, circumference and grip are separate charts on one eight-week axis with their own scales; readings outside it are counted, not drawn at its edges. "Exercise performance" plots one exercise at a time, matched by catalog id only, from exercises with a reviewed direct mapping and at least two sessions: the top set per session (or the longest hold), in the profile's unit, with sets of unknown arm marked and a session without a load never drawn at zero. It says plainly that a different grip, implement or range of motion is a different line, and nothing says a change was caused by training.
  - **A repeatable protocol.** Add a reading starts from the latest reading of the same measure on the same arm of the region: its landmark, distance (in the profile's unit), posture, condition and device, with one line saying so ("Filled in from your last reading on the right arm (7 Oct): place, posture, condition and tape. Change any you did differently.", as `MeasurementsPanel.render.test.ts` reads it); with no such reading, from the measure's own place and posture. Changing the measure starts from that measure's last reading; changing the arm does too, unless the athlete has already typed a place or posture, which is kept. On the charts, a reading taken at a different landmark, distance or posture from the reading before it is a diamond, not a dot (landmarks compared without case or spacing; distances within half a millimetre), and the scale line counts them ("2 diamonds: readings at a different place or posture than the one before"). A pumped reading is already drawn hollow, so a change of condition alone is not a diamond.
  - **Measured bests** (brief §10, "reward … measured performance milestones without fabricating tissue growth"). The exercise chart rings the session whose top set or longest hold beats every earlier session of that exercise, its whole history included, on what the chart draws, and says it in one line: "Longest hold so far: 80 s, 29 Sep (ringed)", "Heaviest top set so far: …", "Most reps in a top set so far: …". The grip chart does the same for the reading that beats every earlier one of the same arm taken the same way as the latest (place, posture and condition): "Highest grip reading so far: 99.2 lb on a dynamometer, 28 Sep (ringed)", adding "at this place, posture and condition" when other readings were taken another way. A first session or reading has nothing to beat and is never one; a later tie does not take it over. Circumference has no best: a tape takes in every tissue under it, so a larger reading is not a performance and would read as growth. Nothing else is said: no streak, no score, no reason (`progress-performance-*.png`, `progress-readings-*.png`).
- **Access.** Every control is at least 44 × 44 px: a short text action standing on its own (Clear, the notes' **Open**, an exercise's **Sets**, the Suggestions Undo, the Progress Undo) carries `spz-link-box`. A sweep with every disclosure open on the page, on FCU's sheet with every disclosure open, on the no-arm sets sheet, on the wrist flexion movement sheet with every disclosure open, on the add sheet with a day chosen and on a week's bouts from Progress found no control under 44 px at 390, 375 or 320 (`touch-targets` and `touch-targets-sheets` in `checks.json`). The app's toast action and cancel (sonner's `[data-button]`, `index.css`) are at least 44 × 44 px too: the add's Undo measures 59.1 × 44, as do Plan's own "removed" and "moved later" toasts (`toast-*.png`). Inputs are 16 px; the sheet takes focus on its heading, keeps Tab inside, closes on Escape or the scrim and returns focus. **How this is counted** opened from a sheet is the exception: the sheet closes, the method opens with its top just under the sticky tab row, and focus goes to the method's summary rather than back to the row the sheet was opened from (`method-from-sheet-*.png`: the summary at 77.5 px under a tab row ending at 49, with focus on it). The selected muscle and changed totals are announced in a polite live region; motion uses the grammar tokens and stops under reduced motion. Text on the Body Lab blue is on-dark ink: **Back to …**, the strip's links and the window line's pair are underlined in link colour rather than set in it (link ink reads under 4.5:1 on that blue).

"The authors recruited 17 healthy women aged 20–40. Each performed the active straight leg raise with either leg, with and without ankle weights. "During ASLR, the iliacus, rectus femoris, adductor longus and psoas were active ipsilaterally, but psoas was also active contralaterally."

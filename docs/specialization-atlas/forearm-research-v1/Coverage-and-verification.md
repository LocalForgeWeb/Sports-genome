## Important limitations

- Scores are editor-defined ordinal estimates, not measured activation or percentages of growth.
- No per-muscle tendon injury coefficient or validated injury prediction is supplied.
- Rep ranges are practical task defaults; no experimentally established optimum for all 19 individual muscles was found in this targeted synthesis.
- Actual protocol, load, ROM, effort, lever geometry and assistance can change involvement.
- Several listed exercises lack directly applicable studies; their mappings use anatomy and analog evidence.
- Unknown matrix cells do not establish inactivity.
- The reference engine expects authorized, date-filtered, current event revisions from the real app. It deliberately rejects ambiguous parent grouping and unreviewed production mappings.
- The supplied Manus interfaces were checked; the live Sports Genome schema was not.

## 3. Calculation definitions

All attribution calculators apply the supporting weight exactly once, at the moment of attribution, with the single source `logicCalibration.exposure.secondarySetConvention = 0.5` (`lib/evidenceTraceability.ts:214`): `weeklyVolume.ts:48`, `sessionVolume.ts:84`, `recoverySpacing.ts:32`, `weekReview.ts:252`. Nothing downstream re-weights. Within one exercise a muscle tagged both primary and secondary counts once, as direct (`weeklyVolume.ts:38-39`, `sessionVolume.ts:79-82`, `recoverySpacing.ts:30-32`, `weekReview.ts:240-241`); 17 of 400 catalog exercises carry such an overlap.

### 3.1 "344.5 attributed sets across all muscles this week"

- Where: `WeeklyMuscleVolumePanel.tsx:39` (`attributedTotal = Σ equivalentSets` over all rows), printed at `:73`; `weekReview.ts:317` (`attributedTotal`), shown only in the methodology paragraph `WeekReviewBoard.tsx:175`.
- Numerator: Σ over every exercise on every saved day of sets × (|primaryMuscles| + 0.5 × |secondaryMuscles \ primaryMuscles|). Includes rows the old panel never rendered (rank 9+, `slice(4, 8)` at `:31-32`).
- Denominator: none. The only comparator is `workSets` (`weekReview.ts:243`, `:310` area), the sets to perform.
- Scope: week, the active week's saved non-empty days of the current split (`visibleDayPlan`, `trainingDayPlan.ts:156-166`).
- Algorithm: `weeklyVolume.ts:35-55`: sets = leading integer of the saved prescription, else of `getGoalPrescription(goal, index)`, else 3; direct += sets per primary muscle; support += `Number((sets × 0.5).toFixed(1))` per secondary-not-primary; `equivalentSets = direct + support`; rows sorted by `equivalentSets` desc then label.
- Constants: 0.5 (`evidenceTraceability.ts:214`); fallback 3 (literal at `weeklyVolume.ts:37`; `workoutReview.defaultPrescriptionSets` `:248`; `sessionVolume.defaultSetsPerExercise` `:52`).
- Brief verdict (§8): methodology only. The arithmetic is correct and pinned (`weekReview.test.ts:35-52`); the number grows with how many muscles the catalog tags (abs is a secondary tag on 192/400 exercises, forearms on 115), not with work. The board shows `workSets` as the week headline; the committed panel at `@18744d3` printed the total as its closing line.

### 3.2 Per-muscle totals ("Glutes 37, abs 36, upper back 29.5, forearms 27")

- Where: `weeklyVolume.ts:55` (`equivalentSets`), `WeeklyMuscleVolumePanel.tsx:44-45`; `weekReview.ts:278` (`total: round1(direct + supporting)`), `WeekReviewBoard.tsx:232-234`.
- Numerator: for one catalog key, Σ sets of exercises tagging it primary × 1.0 + Σ sets of exercises tagging it secondary-not-primary × 0.5, over the week.
- Denominator: none for the number. Bars: old panel `max = Math.max(12, …equivalentSets)` (`:24`, literal 12); board `analysis.max[metric]` with floor 1 (`WeekReviewBoard.tsx:69`; `weekReview.ts:313`).
- Scope: week, one key per row, no hierarchy. `shoulders`, `frontDelts`, `sideDelts`, `rearDelts`, `upperBack`, `lats`, `traps` are each their own row; 75 of 400 exercises tag a group key and a specific key together. `muscleVocabulary.ts:21-25`: `upperBack` = rhomboids + mid trapezius, a sibling of lats and traps, not a parent; `shoulders` is a true umbrella (`anatomyRegions.ts:35-38`).
- Algorithm: as 3.1 per key. Rows are created on first encounter (`weeklyVolume.ts:41,47`), so an untrained muscle is absent and "Not planned" (`:59`) is unreachable; `analyzeWeek` adds zero rows for the split targets of the week's slots (`weekReview.ts:268-271`).
- Constants: 0.5; 12 bar floor (old panel only).
- Verdict: keep as primary with the unit split visible ("N direct · M supporting (est.)"), default metric "Direct sets" (`WeekReviewBoard.tsx:57`); the single blended figure must not be the row's headline. Fixture (e): Rectus abdominis 0 direct + 12.5 supporting ranked 4th of all muscles.

### 3.3 By-day values ("Pull 5.5, Legs 20, Sport Transfer 11.5")

- Where: `weeklyVolume.ts:43,50` (`daySets[dayKey]`), rendered at `WeeklyMuscleVolumePanel.tsx:53` only when more than one day contributes; `weekReview.ts:246,254` (direct and supporting kept apart per day) and `:276-277` (every built session present, zero included), rendered as bars in MuscleDetail `WeekReviewBoard.tsx:246-259`.
- Numerator: for one muscle and one plan day, Σ direct sets + Σ 0.5 × supporting sets of that day's exercises.
- Denominator: old panel none; MuscleDetail scales each day bar by the muscle's own week value for the chosen metric (`WeekReviewBoard.tsx:247`).
- Scope: week → one plan day (`${index}-${day}`, `trainingDayPlan.ts:51`; label after the first "-", `WeeklyMuscleVolumePanel.tsx:6`).
- Algorithm: same attribution; Σ over days equals the row total (pinned `weekReview.test.ts:35-52, 101-108`). The old panel hid the breakdown for a single day and named the day in the header (`:37-38,62`).
- Verdict: keep as primary (drilldown), direct and supporting apart; the inline "attributed" list is the methodology form.

### 3.5 "23 work sets in Week 1 · Day 02 · Pull"

- Where: `workoutPlanner.ts:70` (`totalSets`), `parseNumber` `:52`; `ProgrammingGuidePanel.tsx:17`; `WorkoutHealthPanel.tsx:18`.

- Scope: day (`customWorkout`, `dayPrescriptions`). The week counterpart is `weekReview.workSets`, computed with `parseSetCount`.
- Algorithm: `parseNumber` is `/\d+/` anywhere; the week calculators use `/^\s*(\d+)/` leading (`weeklyVolume.ts:30`, `sessionVolume.ts:55`, `recoverySpacing.ts:22`, `weekReview.ts:237`). - Verdict: keep as primary for Day scope, labelled with the day (the Planning guide already is); never shown as or beside a week figure. The two parsers should become one; which reading is right is undefined (owner decision).

### 3.6 "161 total effort"

- Where: `workoutPlanner.ts:75` (`sessionLoad = Math.round(totalSets × averageRpe)`), `averageRpe` `:73`; `WorkoutHealthPanel.tsx:18-19`.
- Numerator: `totalSets` × mean over the day's exercises of `parseNumber(settings[id].rpe, 7)`.
- Denominator: none; no scale, no upper bound.
- Scope: day.
- Algorithm: with untouched settings every exercise contributes RPE 7, so the figure is 7 × planned sets. Fixture: 23 sets → 161 (`sessionLoad === round(totalSets × 7)` confirmed); RPE 9 on every exercise → 207; empty day → 0.
- Constants: `workoutReview.defaultRpe` 7 (`evidenceTraceability.ts:250`); `defaultSettings` `workoutPlanner.ts:40`.
- Verdict: remove from any main board; if kept, Day-review detail only, labelled "sets × average RPE (default 7)". No test pins the value. The committed panel now prints "total effort: sets × average RPE" and the sentence at `WorkoutHealthPanel.tsx:19`.

### 3.7 "24% muscle overlap"

- Where: `exerciseGenome.ts:270-278` (`getWorkoutGenome.redundancy`), `similarity` `:224-232`, Jaccard `overlap` `:219-222`; returned through `workoutPlanner.ts:66,88`; `WorkoutHealthPanel.tsx:18`.
- Numerator: Σ over every unordered pair of the day's exercises of `similarity = clamp(100 × (0.4 × J(primary ∪ secondary muscles) + 0.26 × J(genome movementPatterns) + 0.14 × (same resistanceProfile.bias ? 1 : 0.25) + 0.2 × J(qualities)))`.
- Denominator: C(n, 2) pairs; 0 below two exercises (`emptyWorkoutRedundancy`); clamp 0–100.
- Scope: day; prescriptions, sets and settings are not inputs.
- Algorithm: fixture six-exercise Pull day → 15 pairs, mean 52 → "52%"; the recording-shaped Pull day [57, 43, 87, 126] → 38%; one exercise → 0. Muscles carry at most 40 of 100 points and the muscle term is an unweighted Jaccard; the 0.5 weight plays no part. An exercise with no muscle tags but Bench's genome still scores 60 against Bench.
- Constants: `muscleSimilarityWeight` 0.4, `movementSimilarityWeight` 0.26, `resistanceProfileSimilarityWeight` 0.14, `qualitySimilarityWeight` 0.2 (`evidenceTraceability.ts:87-90`), `resistanceProfileMismatchSimilarity` 0.25 (`:115`), `emptyWorkoutRedundancy` 0 (`:116`); prompt threshold `workoutReview.highRedundancyReview` 62 (`:251`; `workoutPlanner.ts:78`), with a namesake `exerciseGenome.highRedundancyReview` 62 (`:74`).
- Verdict: remove from the primary overview; Day-review detail only, renamed away from "muscle", without the % sign, with the definition beside it. Not a week metric. No test pins the percentage.

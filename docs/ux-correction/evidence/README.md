# Evidence — UX correction (§16 journeys, §18 evidence set)

Everything here was produced against the built app (`npx vite build`, served by `npx vite preview --port 4173`) in headless Chromium through Playwright, on the commit this folder ships with. It is **browser emulation, not a physical iPhone**: viewports are 390×844 unless stated, the "keyboard" case is a 420px-tall viewport, and "larger text" is a 20px or 32px root font applied to `html`. Toasts were allowed to clear before each screenshot except where the toast is the evidence. The probe scripts are in `../probes/`.

## Further checks (`../probes/extras.mjs`, `overflow32.mjs`, `phasef.mjs`, `phasee.mjs`)

- **Contrast (A11Y-07):** every visible text run in the first viewport of Home, Plan, Workout (live), Matches, Exercises, Strength and Profile was checked against its nearest solid background, or against every stop of its gradient background (the worst stop counts). After lifting the subtle/faint text tones, the link blue and the positive green, and giving the active day tab white text, no run is below 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text).
- **Rapid tab switching (STATE-10):** tapping Plan → Review → Workout → Matches in one tick leaves Matches selected with "Exercise matches", its URL and title, and no Plan or Review content; the reverse leaves Plan.
- **200% text (A11Y-03):** at 360px with a 32px root font the tab rows overflow with arrows and the selected tab stays in view; the selected day tab scrolls into view; the page's own width stays 360 on Review, Workout, Matches, Muscles, Exercises, Progress and Strength. See the checklist note on A11Y-03 for what remains.
- **Widths (A11Y-01/02/04/05/06/14/15):** 320, 360 (20px root), 390, 430 and 1280: no horizontal page overflow, no interactive target under 44px wide or tall in the sampled controls, and with the resume strip up the last row on every page sits above it.
- **Desktop (A11Y-17):** the brand row, tab row and content share one 68rem column; paragraph lines stop at 1088px.

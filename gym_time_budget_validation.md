# Gym-Time Budget Validation

The Home workspace now includes a selectable gym-time budget with 30, 45, 60, 75, and 90+ minute options. The selected value is stored with the athlete profile.

Live validation confirmed that selecting **30 minutes** immediately changes the planning guidance to an essential session, caps the recommended stack at three exercises, and shows the associated priority and rest cue. The original 60-minute selection presents a balanced five-exercise session scope. This control remains visible alongside sport, goal, and training-days settings.

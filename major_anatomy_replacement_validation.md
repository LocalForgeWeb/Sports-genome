# Major Anatomy Replacement Validation

The live Body Lab now displays original, high-detail muscular anatomy figures instead of the former flat geometric body drawing. The anterior and posterior illustrations are cropped into their respective boards, retain clickable SVG hotspot overlays, and sit between sparse exterior callout rails. The desktop board preserves the requested centered-figure and flanking-label hierarchy, while the existing mobile rules stack the figures before the label cards.

The project passes TypeScript checking and a production build after the replacement.

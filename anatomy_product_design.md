# Blue-and-Gold Anatomy Product Design

The detailed atlas will remain an original vector surface model. The user-supplied image serves only as a high-level interaction reference: it demonstrates that a useful training anatomy tool needs a **front/back switch**, individually labeled regions, and a readable relationship between a highlighted shape and a full muscle name. The app will not copy the image, watermark, layout, illustration, or labels.

The visual system will use deep navy and performance blue as primary product surfaces, with **gold** for the GO mark, secondary signal accents, and named-muscle callouts. White and pale blue will keep dense anatomy information readable. Interactive muscle regions will follow one color grammar: inactive outline, selected gold fill, primary exercise relevance blue fill, and deep/underlying anatomy shown with a dashed or translucent treatment.

The paste-stack flow will accept one exercise per line in simple forms such as `Barbell Bench Press`, `Barbell Bench Press — 3 x 8`, and `Barbell Bench Press | 3 sets | 8 reps | RPE 8`. It will normalize case and punctuation, identify exact and close catalog matches, preserve parsed programming metadata, and require confirmation before importing. Unmatched lines remain visible so the athlete can correct or skip them.

Programming controls will be per exercise and include **sets**, **repetitions or duration**, **load or effort (RPE)**, **rest**, and **notes**. The schedule quiz and planning logic will accept **1–7 days per week**; one day becomes a full-body priority session, while higher frequencies increase the number of proposed training exposures without making a medical or performance guarantee.

# Gym Optimizer Feature Tutorial

The expanded tutorial will use task-oriented chapters rather than a brief product tour. Each step will answer three questions: **where to go**, **what to do there**, and **why it matters for the current plan**. The guide will begin with profile setup and continue through sport context, recommendations, Custom Builder, routine import, weekly planning, Body Lab, Movement Atlas, and Exercise Genome.

The quiz selection indicator will move to the card’s vertical midpoint on the right edge, while the title and description reserve enough inline space to prevent collision. Technical labels remain at the top and bottom edges; the selection check occupies its own protected lane.

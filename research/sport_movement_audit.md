# Sport-Movement Evidence Audit

## Interpretation Rule

The movement database describes **observable actions and likely phase-dependent contributors**. It does not claim direct muscle activation, fixed prime-mover rankings for every athlete, injury prediction, or guaranteed transfer from a gym exercise to sport performance.

| Area | Applied data correction or expansion | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Wrestling | Corrected sprawl wording and added **gut-wrench turn** and **upper-body throw / hip-toss pattern**. | Gym work may overlap with bracing, attachment, turning, or force-direction qualities; partner response and safe technique remain sport skills. |
| Soccer | Corrected throw-in sequencing so it is treated as coordinated, legal bilateral overhead projection rather than a fixed muscle sequence. | Contribution varies with individual technique and phase. |
| Track & field | Corrected the block-start record where body-action, muscle, stabilizer, and family fields were misassigned. | Sled/resisted starts can share horizontal-projection qualities but do not guarantee sprint transfer. |
| Swimming | Corrected breaststroke-pull joint-action wording into phase-dependent outsweep, insweep, and recovery mechanics. | Listed arm and shoulder contributors are not a direct EMG ranking. |
| Volleyball | Added **reactive readiness step**, covering the preparatory low stance and first-step response gap. | Perception, ball reading, and tactical choice are not reproduced by gym drills. |
| Boxing | Added **angle step / cut-off step**, covering lateral-diagonal line change and guarded reorientation. | Timing, distance management, and opponent reading remain boxing skills. |
| Ice hockey | Added **goalie shuffle / T-push transition**, covering goalie lateral repositioning and recovery. | Skate-edge mechanics and puck tracking are goalie-specific. |
| Brazilian jiu-jitsu | Added **standing clinch and takedown-defense cycle**, connecting stance, pummeling, hip denial, and re-stand mechanics. | Grips, timing, and opponent response remain grappling skills. |

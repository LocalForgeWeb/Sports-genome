# Batch 2: Verified High-Priority Findings

## Interpretation rule

The records below are source-verified bibliography and bounded findings for product reconciliation. They support **conditional biomechanics context** and **model-uncertainty disclosures**. They do not authorize athlete-specific force, tendon-property, activation, injury-risk, or performance predictions.

| PMID | Verified source | Bounded finding | Safe Sports Genome use | Key limitation |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 18972175 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18972175/) | In the studied cadaveric lower limbs, force-related architectural characteristics and excursion-related characteristics differed meaningfully across muscles. | Population-level lower-limb architecture context and uncertainty ranges. | Elderly formaldehyde-fixed limbs are not living-athlete measurements. |
| 21502118 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21502118/) | Fibre length relates to excursion and PCSA relates to maximal force context; force also depends on length and shortening velocity. | Explain why architecture and operating state are inputs, not standalone outcomes. | Narrative review; no personal prediction. |
| 33935234 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33935234/) | In recreationally active men, quadriceps volume related strongly to maximal knee-extension torque, while adding architecture-derived PCSA did not improve that relationship. | Avoid presenting size, PCSA, or architecture as direct force measurement. | Cross-sectional knee-extension study in one population. |
| 23998280 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23998280/) | Moment-arm methods and coordinate/coupling assumptions can materially change modelled muscle-force solutions. | Explicitly label moment-arm methods as a source of model uncertainty. | Conceptual 2D methods study, not athlete-force validation. |
| 7775488 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7775488/) | Elbow flexion/extension and pronation/supination leverage varied with elbow and forearm position. | Keep upper-limb leverage position-dependent rather than fixed. | Two anatomic specimens and a geometry model. |
| 10828324 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10828324/) | Elbow capacity reflected the interaction of architecture and moment arm, not size alone. | Explain multi-factor elbow contribution context. | Ten specimens; estimated capacity, not sport performance. |
| 30411350 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30411350/) | Experimental shoulder leverage patterns varied by muscle subregion, elevation plane, and joint position. | Population-level shoulder movement interpretation and uncertainty priors. | Heterogeneous non-sport-specific native-shoulder evidence. |
| 31668905 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31668905/) | Static optimization under-recruited several antagonist shoulder muscles compared with EMG-driven modeling, changing joint-force estimates. | Preserve co-contraction/model-dependent warning for shoulder estimates. | Four healthy adults in submaximal tasks. |
| 16871004 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16871004/) | Tendon compliance changes fascicle behavior and elastic energy exchange in dynamic movement. | Describe tendon as conditional movement context, not a personal tendon estimate. | Review; not a predictive cohort. |
| 22507351 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22507351/) | Gait-model predictions were sensitive to musculotendon parameters, especially tendon slack length, with muscle-specific effects. | Emphasize sensitivity and parameter uncertainty in lower-limb model outputs. | Gait simulation based on five healthy men. |

## Reconciliation decision

The existing model already states that moment arms, architecture, force-length/velocity context, and co-contraction are conditional inferences rather than personal measurements. The defensible addition is therefore **traceable source metadata on those factor explanations**, not new numerical score weights. That preserves the current evidence hierarchy: direct longitudinal exercise evidence takes precedence over conditional mechanics ranking.

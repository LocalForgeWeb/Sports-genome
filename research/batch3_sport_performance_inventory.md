# Batch 3 Inventory: Sport-Specific Performance Demands and Training Transfer

## Scope

The uploaded batch provides a sport-to-program reasoning framework:

> Sport → position, event, or style → competition movement and energy demands → force/velocity demands → trainable qualities → exercise or conditioning selection → bounded sport transfer.

The source explicitly rejects universal whole-sport profiles when role, event, style, weight class, competition level, surface, match duration, or tournament structure materially changes the demand.

## Product constraint

Sport-specific evidence can prioritize qualities and make the exercise pool more context-sensitive. It cannot create a universal athlete profile, guarantee sport performance, quantify an individual competition demand without direct measurement, or turn association evidence into a causal exercise prescription.

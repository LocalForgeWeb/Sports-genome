The public Strength Level standards page describes 287 exercises and a large community-submitted dataset, but its terms prohibit automated or programmatic access without explicit written authorization. Sports Genome must not scrape, copy, or derive tables from that service absent a written license or approved access path.[1] [2]

The powerlifting reference by van den Hoek et al. is a distinct source rather than a universal gym-lift standard. It is based on drug-tested, unequipped competitive entries, and reports relative squat, bench press, and deadlift values by sex, age class, and weight class.[3] A generic gym lift must remain **unavailable** against this reference unless every source condition is recorded and matched.

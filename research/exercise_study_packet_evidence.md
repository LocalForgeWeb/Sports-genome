# Exercise Study Packet: Verified Evidence Map

## Scope

The supplied exercise-science packet contains 100 citations organized into seven groups: exercise selection and range of motion; lower-body biomechanics; posterior chain; pressing; pulling and modality; unilateral/bilateral transfer; and power/weightlifting derivatives. The project retains the detailed verification output in `verify_exercise_science_packet.json`; this document records the rules that are safe to translate into catalog mechanics.

## Evidence-weight rule

Longitudinal MRI, ultrasound, muscle-volume, cross-sectional-area, strength-transfer, and direct biomechanics outcomes take precedence over acute surface EMG. EMG remains useful for a narrow exercise-configuration or activation-context note, but it is not treated as proof of hypertrophy, exact force, sport transfer, or individual response.

## High-confidence catalog implications

| Area | Source-supported implication | Boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| Hamstring curls | Seated knee-flexion training can be tagged as a potentially stronger whole- and biarticular-hamstring growth option than prone curls in the studied protocol. | Maeo et al. 2021, 12-week within-participant MRI study, PMID [33009197](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33009197/); do not claim an individual guarantee. |
| Triceps extensions | Overhead elbow extension can receive a long-head lengthened-position note and a stronger direct hypertrophy evidence tag than neutral-arm extension. | Maeo et al. 2023, 12-week within-participant MRI study, PMID [35819335](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35819335/). |
| Calf raises | Standing calf raises can receive a gastrocnemius-bias note; seated work remains a viable soleus contributor. | Kinoshita et al. 2023, 12-week MRI study, PMID [38156065](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38156065/). |
| Squat and hip thrust | Both are viable glute-growth contributors; squat carries a stronger quadriceps/adductor inclusion note, and strength change remains exercise-specific. | Plotkin et al. 2023, randomized MRI intervention, PMID [37877099](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37877099/). |
| Range of motion | Full ROM, long-length partial ROM, short-length partial ROM, and individualized ROM must be distinct descriptors. No universal `deep is always superior` or `lengthened partials always win` rule is allowed. | Bloomquist et al. 2013, PMID [23604798](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23604798/); Larsen et al. 2025, PMID [40113586](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40113586); Gschneidner et al. 2025, PMID [41055237](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41055237). |
| Hamstring pattern | Nordic work is knee-flexion/lengthened-eccentric biased; stiff-leg/Romanian deadlift variants are hip-hinge/posterior-chain patterns. Regional response is variable and direct growth evidence outranks EMG. | RCT, PMID [40586278](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40586278); EMG/systematic-review context: PMIDs [24149748](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24149748/) and [32107499](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32107499). |
| Pressing | Bench angle, grip, stability, and velocity are mechanical descriptors. Acute EMG is not a direct hypertrophy score; regional adaptations are conditional. | Joint-moment study, PMID [33555823](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33555823); regional pectoralis intervention, PMID [36334406](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36334406). |
| Rows and modality | Unilateral rows can carry a trunk-demand descriptor. Machines and free weights use modality-specific strength-transfer tags but neutral default hypertrophy equivalence where effort, volume, and targeting are comparable. | Unilateral row EMG, PMID [26134664](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26134664); matched modality trial, PMID [37535335](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37535335); modality meta-analysis, PMID [34609100](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34609100). |
| Unilateral/bilateral work | Treat unilateral and bilateral work as partially transferable, not interchangeable. Transfer depends on movement and the tested task. | Rugby interventions, PMIDs [30688873](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30688873), [30844983](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30844983), and [26200193](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26200193). |

## Provisional or counterevidence rules

Several recent or incompletely resolved citations remain provisional. The catalog must not elevate a study to a universal rule when the original study uses a short intervention, narrow population, selected measurement sites, acute activation, modeled muscle force, or indirect sport transfer. Explicit counterevidence—including trained-leg-press ROM results and large full-ROM versus lengthened-partial equivalence findings—must remain visible in the evidence register.

# Batch 3: Verified High-Priority Sport-Performance Findings

## Product interpretation rule

These records support sport-, role-, event-, style-, and competition-context discussions. They do **not** establish individual talent, genetics, guaranteed exercise transfer, or a universal profile for an entire sport.

| Sport | PMID | Verified source | Bounded finding | Product-safe use | Key limitation |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Wrestling | 28030533 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28030533/) | Higher-level wrestling is discussed through a mixed aerobic-recovery, anaerobic, maximal, isometric, explosive, and strength-endurance demand context. | Explain multi-domain wrestling preparation and preserve style/weight/competition context. | Heterogeneous narrative review; no individual causal prediction. |
| American football | 37050597 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37050597/) | Monitoring literature emphasized external-load and catch-skill applications while internal-load evidence remained a gap. | Present monitoring context and evidence gaps, not one fixed football prescription. | Heterogeneous scoping review, not a position-performance trial. |
| Basketball | 29039018 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29039018/) | Guards generally performed more sprinting and high-intensity shuffling than forwards and centers across the reviewed evidence. | Support guard/forward/center movement-volume context. | Group-level observational match demand. |
| Soccer | 29199782 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29199782/) | Male soccer evidence differed by competitive level, position, and age across aerobic, speed, strength, power, and agility measures. | Keep soccer goal, position, and competition level explicit. | Male group evidence; not causal or individually predictive. |
| Swimming | 26839618 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26839618/) | Physiological responses varied with training background and experience; evidence did not support a fixed universal marker. | Frame swimmer monitoring as individualized response context. | Diverse adult elite-swimmer longitudinal studies; no stroke/distance prescription. |
| Tennis | 36752978 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36752978/) | Match demands were intermittent and multidirectional and varied by sex, level, and surface. | Inform contextual tennis conditioning while retaining surface and player context. | Aggregated observational estimates with substantial variation. |
| Volleyball | 41460726 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41460726/) | Jump volume and jump height varied by position and sex. | Offer position- and sex-contextualized workload discussion. | Heterogeneous match-load synthesis. |
| Boxing | 35380916 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35380916/) | Acute responses generally differed among competition, sparring, and simulations. | Separate bout, sparring, and simulation recovery context. | Amateur-boxing acute-response evidence only. |
| Mixed martial arts | 26993133 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26993133/) | Grappling- and striking-oriented success profiles emphasized different strength, power, and anaerobic contexts. | Preserve strategy-sensitive MMA modifier context. | Heterogeneous adult male combat-sport review. |
| Brazilian jiu-jitsu | 28194734 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28194734/) | Strength measures related to success more consistently than VO2max; anaerobic and power evidence remained limited. | Use explicit evidence-gap language around BJJ conditioning priorities. | Predominantly male gi studies with limited sport-specific evidence. |
| Rugby union | 41359906 | [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41359906/) | Evidence showed heterogeneous testing and no consensus on a single essential battery. | Use game-model and positional context rather than universal rugby benchmarks. | Heterogeneous review; no causal or individual prediction. |

## Reconciliation expectation

Existing profiles should only gain source metadata, context clarifications, or restrained quality-priority explanations where the current model does not already express the verified boundary. No new fixed athlete score, universal sport ranking, or automatic plan rewrite is justified by this pass alone.

# Clarified Anatomy Board Layout

On desktop, the anatomy board is a **two-column comparison**. Each half holds one centered full-length figure. Callout cards occupy the left and right perimeter zones within that half, and thin connector lines extend inward toward the related muscle region. The result prioritizes immediate visual correspondence between label and anatomy, rather than a grid of labels that competes with the figure.

The anterior and posterior figures retain separate headers and reset controls. Cards use the original Gym Optimizer role and relevance system, including full labels and click selection. Connector lines are representative interaction cues rather than anatomical measurements.

On mobile, the card perimeter is intentionally removed to prevent overlap. A compact centered figure appears first, followed by its related callout cards in a two-column list. The front board precedes the back board so the experience remains linear and legible.

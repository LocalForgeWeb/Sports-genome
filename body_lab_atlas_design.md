# Body Lab Atlas Redesign

The redesigned Body Lab uses a **three-panel desktop layout**. The left panel owns filters, layers, thresholds, search, and optional comparison controls. The center panel owns the large single-view anatomy heat map. The right panel is intentionally quiet until a muscle is selected, at which point it becomes a focused inspector.

The atlas does not use permanent labels around the body. Hover exposes a concise tooltip; click locks selection, dims non-selected regions, and opens the selected-muscle detail. The heat scale communicates only standardized involvement. Muscle role is communicated through filters, badges, and inspector text.

On mobile, the order is exercise summary, compact controls, large anatomy canvas, and a selected-muscle bottom sheet. The involved-muscles table stays collapsed until requested. Comparison and range-of-motion controls are advanced tools and do not appear in the default first view.

# Dual-View Anatomy Board Design

The new Body Lab centerpiece will show **front and back anatomy simultaneously** inside one original blue-and-white analysis board. The supplied reference establishes the interaction pattern—not a source image or asset to copy: readable anterior and posterior silhouettes, surrounding labeled cards, connector lines, and a concise role/intensity grammar.

Each side will show a compact original vector figure with selected exercise regions colored by role. Callout cards will contain the **full anatomical label**, the training role (Primary, Supporting, or Stabilizing), and the relevance level (High, Moderate, or Low). Connector lines will visually associate a card with a muscle region. Clicking a region or its card will open the existing inspector context and retain the exercise/sport links.

On desktop, cards remain beside each figure for rapid comparison. On mobile, callout cards move below the respective figure and preserve the full label, role, and level without requiring the user to switch front/back views. Deep regions are still available as subtle labels but are visually deprioritized to keep the board clean.

# Body Lab Evidence Boundaries

The anatomy heat map will communicate a **standardized involvement estimate**, not a direct measurement of muscle force, hypertrophy, or injury risk. Surface electromyography can indicate aspects of muscle electrical activity but has established limits when used to infer force or compare across muscles, so the interface will retain confidence labels and an expandable evidence note rather than implying laboratory precision.[1][2]

Heat-map values will combine exercise mechanics, named joint actions, muscle roles, and the available exercise-specific evidence. The selected-muscle inspector will distinguish primary, supporting, and stabilizing roles in text while reserving body color for one signal only: estimated involvement. This keeps the visual model interpretable and aligns with the user’s requested single heat-scale hierarchy.

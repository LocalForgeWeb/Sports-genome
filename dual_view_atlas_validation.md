# Dual-View Anatomy Board Validation

The live Body Lab displays the new original anatomy board with front and back muscle figures simultaneously. The browser exposes both figure paths and surrounding callout controls, including full labels such as clavicular pectoralis major, anterior deltoid, infraspinatus, latissimus dorsi, and triceps brachii heads. Each callout carries a role and relevance level, and each muscle region remains clickable.

The Body Lab layout was then adjusted to allocate the dual-view board a full-width inspection bay on wider desktop screens. This avoids compressing the callouts and gives each anatomy side enough room for readable labels, connector cues, and a selected-muscle inspector.

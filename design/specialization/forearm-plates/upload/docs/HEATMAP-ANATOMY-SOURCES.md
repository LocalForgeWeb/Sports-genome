# 2D forearm heatmap anatomy sources

This implementation uses a front/palmar panel and a back/dorsal panel. The SVG is a functional training heatmap, not a clinical dissection atlas: every listed Sports Genome muscle has its own selectable region and host-data fill; deep regions use a small `deep` badge and slightly inset drawing treatment rather than pretending they are fully visible through the superficial layer.

- The anterior compartment is organized as superficial flexors/pronators, the intermediate flexor digitorum superficialis, and deep flexor digitorum profundus, flexor pollicis longus, and pronator quadratus. The anterior layout therefore places pronator teres proximally on a diagonal, FCR on the radial side, palmaris longus centrally, FCU on the ulnar side, FDS as the broad central mass, FDP deep/ulnar, FPL deep/radial, and pronator quadratus as the distal transverse region. [1] [2]
- The posterior layout places ECRL/ECRB on the radial lateral side, extensor digitorum centrally, EDM and ECU toward the ulnar side, anconeus posterolaterally near the elbow, and the deep posterior thumb/index group as distinct inset regions. [3]
- The deep posterior group is supinator, abductor pollicis longus, extensor pollicis brevis, extensor pollicis longus, and extensor indicis. APL begins distal to the supinator; EPL follows a more medial oblique distal course; EPB is radial/distal; and EI is narrow and distal. [3] [4]

The diagram intentionally keeps Brachioradialis as an outer/lateral landmark on the front/lateral border even though its origin/innervation are grouped with the posterior extensor compartment; its elbow-flexion function is distinct. [3]

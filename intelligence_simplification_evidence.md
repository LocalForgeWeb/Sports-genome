# Exercise Intelligence and Simplification Evidence

## Data additions

The exercise catalog will expose concise, pattern-level coaching notes derived from each record's existing movement, equipment, primary-muscle, quality, and sport-transfer fields. These notes are intended as selection and execution prompts, not individualized treatment or performance claims.

The guidance uses a small movement-pattern vocabulary so the product can lead with the dominant training action rather than listing every synergist by default. A 2024 review describes vertical push, horizontal push, vertical pull, horizontal pull, hip, knee, ankle, and vertebral-column categories as a more accessible way to organize resistance training [1]. The NSCA describes common resistance exercises as loaded variations of generalizable multi-joint movement patterns and emphasizes exercise selection, instruction, monitoring, feedback, and progression [2].

Cable records receive a clear "adjustable line of pull" context note rather than an inflated superiority claim. A controlled comparison of selected cable and selectorized exercises reported differences in muscle activity, joint angles, and range of motion, illustrating that equipment can alter the movement context and can be selected for specific motor or biomechanical patterns [3].

## UI decision

Primary cards will show one conclusion, one concise coaching cue, and one clear action. Full score components, all muscle roles, mechanics, overlap flags, programming cautions, and sources remain available in existing expandable disclosures. This preserves the dataset without presenting every detail at once.

## Validation

The streamlined workout diagnostics now leads with working sets, estimated duration, overlap, and one actionable coaching cue. Goal bands, pattern balance, time budget, and secondary prompts are behind a single planning-detail disclosure. Movement coverage now shows the current percentage and one next priority before deeper requirements. The legacy duplicated custom-builder surface is hidden; the current programming workspace remains the active builder. Mobile review confirmed the account-first decision flow remains readable with clear option separation.

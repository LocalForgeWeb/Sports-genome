## muscle_mapper — anatomical vector geometry

The Body Lab anatomy in `client/src/components/anatomy/figureGeometry.ts` is
adapted from the vector anatomy in **muscle_mapper** by Surya Mouly.

- Source: <https://github.com/suryamolly/muscle_mapper>

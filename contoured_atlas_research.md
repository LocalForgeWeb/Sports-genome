# Contoured Anatomy Atlas Research Notes

The original SVG atlas will use anatomically distinct regions rather than generic blocks. The shoulder will separate anterior, middle, and posterior deltoid fibers; the posterior shoulder will preserve a scapular arrangement for supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor, trapezius, and latissimus dorsi. The trunk will keep pectoralis major portions, serratus slips, rectus abdominis segments, external oblique, and lats as distinct regions. The posterior atlas will model trapezius as a broad triangular distribution and the latissimus dorsi as a tapered back region.

The atlas treats heat-map values as **standardized involvement estimates**, not direct muscle-force or EMG measurements. Role badges and evidence notes remain separate from the continuous heat scale.
